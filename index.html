Research Framework

Four Pillars of Analysis.

01 — INDUSTRY

Structural Dynamics

Understanding how industries evolve, where value migrates, and which players are structurally advantaged over time.

02 — BUSINESS

Model Durability

Assessing the resilience of business models against competitive pressure, cost structure changes, and regulatory shifts.

03 — CAPITAL

Allocation Behaviour

How management deploys capital — reinvestment, dividends, acquisitions — reveals more than any single financial metric.

04 — RISK

Downside Analysis

Every thesis stress-tested against bear cases, tail risks, and scenarios the market has not yet priced in.

05 — POSITIONING

Competitive Landscape

Where a company sits within its ecosystem and whether that position compounds or erodes over a 5-10 year horizon.

06 — CONVICTION

Position Sizing

Disciplined sizing based on asymmetry, margin of safety, and the quality of the underlying thesis — not emotion.
